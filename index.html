Our Story

The Challenge We Address

Cambodia is an agricultural country, yet smallholder farmers face post-harvest losses, low prices, and limited market access. In Pailin, despite producing world-class longan, up to 50% is wasted each harvest.

Pailin, known for its fertile soils and unique longan orchards, illustrates this paradox. Despite producing premium fruit, up to 50% of longan is wasted in peak harvests. Farmers receive as little as €0.40/kg, while urban youth consume imported sugary drinks instead of local fruit-based alternatives.

Paibloom's Vision

Train farmers on regenerative agroforestry techniques.
Establish pilot processing hubs for longan products.
Implement a local traceability system.
Reduce post-harvest waste and increase farmer incomes.
Introduce healthier snack and beverage options using longan's natural sweetness.
Engage youth and women in developing food products rooted in traditional crops.
